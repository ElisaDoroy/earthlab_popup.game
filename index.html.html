How to Play

Each level represents a major stage in Earth's history. Add ingredients to the experiment tray. When you think you have the correct combination, press CREATE STAGE. Correct combinations unlock the next level.

You can experiment freely: adding extra ingredients is allowed, but only the required ingredients count toward a successful recipe.
